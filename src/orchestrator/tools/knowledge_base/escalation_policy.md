# Escalation Policy

## When to escalate
Escalate to a human support lead when a customer explicitly asks for a manager, when legal or safety concerns are raised, when a refund exceeds the agent approval limit, or when the assistant is not confident in its answer.

## How escalation works
The assistant pauses the conversation and creates an approval request in the support inbox. A human reviewer can approve, reject, or edit the proposed action. The conversation resumes automatically once the reviewer has decided.

## Response targets
Urgent escalations are answered within 1 hour during business hours. Normal escalations are answered within 1 business day.
