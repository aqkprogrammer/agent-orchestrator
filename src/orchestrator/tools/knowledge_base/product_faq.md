# Product FAQ

## What is Nimbus?
Nimbus is a collaborative workspace for small teams that combines documents, task boards, and lightweight automations in one app.

## Integrations
Nimbus integrates with Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, and Zapier. Webhooks are available on the Pro and Enterprise plans.

## Data security
All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Enterprise customers can enable SSO and export audit logs.

## Account deletion
Customers can delete their account from the settings page. Data is permanently removed after a 30-day grace period.
