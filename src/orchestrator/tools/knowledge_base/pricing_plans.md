# Pricing Plans

## Starter
The Starter plan costs $19 per month and includes 3 projects, 10 GB of storage, and email support.

## Pro
The Pro plan costs $49 per month and includes unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage, priority support, team workspaces for up to 10 members, and advanced analytics.

## Enterprise
The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and includes single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, a dedicated account manager, a 99.95% uptime SLA, and unlimited storage.

## Billing
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Starter and Pro. Plans can be upgraded at any time; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
