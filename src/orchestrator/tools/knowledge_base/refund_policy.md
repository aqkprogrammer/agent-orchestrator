# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Items that arrive damaged or defective are always eligible, regardless of the purchase date, as long as the customer reports the damage within 14 days and provides the order number.

## Approval limits
Support agents may issue refunds up to $100 without additional review. Any refund above $100 requires approval from a support lead before money is returned to the customer. Refunds can never exceed the original order total.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Customers receive an email confirmation once the refund has been issued.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards, downloadable software licenses that have been activated, and custom-engraved products are not refundable. Store credit may be offered instead at the support lead's discretion.
