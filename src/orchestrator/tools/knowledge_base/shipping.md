# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental US. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. International orders usually arrive within 7 to 14 business days.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders over $50. Express shipping costs a flat $15. International shipping is calculated at checkout.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, customers can request a replacement or a full refund. Support should look up the order and create a ticket with the carrier tracking number.
